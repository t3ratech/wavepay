## What this means

We use few. The ones that make the site work need no consent; analytics and sharing use cookies you can refuse.

## Strictly necessary

- `wp_theme`: remembers your light, dark or system choice so the page does not flash. Stored for up to a year.
- Language: your language is the page you are on (for example /fr/); no cookie is needed.

## Analytics

Google Analytics 4 measures page views and interactions so we can improve the site. It sets cookies such as `_ga`. You can refuse by turning on Do Not Track or a tracking blocker, or by opting out through Google's browser add-on. We use it for aggregate numbers, not to build profiles.

## Sharing

The share buttons in the footer are provided by ShareThis, which may set its own cookies when the buttons load. Plain share links are used if scripts are blocked.

## Checkout and sign-in

The hosted checkout and your provider's pages set whatever cookies the provider needs; those are governed by the provider's policy.
