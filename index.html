Payments for software, anywhere

One POST registers you. The next POST is a checkout.

Your buyer pays with whatever they actually have: EcoCash, OneMoney, InnBucks, Telecash, O'mari, ZimSwitch, Visa, Mastercard or PayPal — and a licence key lands in their inbox the moment the payment clears. Onboard like an API

No signup form. No sales call. No dashboard tour.

1

Register

POST /v2/register with a name and an email. You get a live mk_live_… merchant key back once — free tier, 25 payments a month, working immediately.

2

Sell

POST /v2/checkouts returns a hosted checkout URL — or push a wallet prompt straight to a phone with paynow-mobile. Idempotent; retries never double-charge.

3

Settle

Signed webhooks or a poll tell you the money landed; a licence key is emailed automatically. Every settlement is in the payments ledger — drain it from your backend.

MCP tools

POST /mcp speaks JSON-RPC: wavepay_register, wavepay_pricing, wavepay_checkout, wavepay_checkout_status, wavepay_catalog. The same internals REST calls — one truth, two wires.

What you get

Checkout is the easy part — WavePay is the rest of it

Every rail a buyer might hold

Zimbabwean wallets covered end to end — EcoCash, OneMoney, InnBucks, Telecash, O'mari — plus ZimSwitch, Visa, Mastercard and PayPal for international buyers. One integration; /v2/providers always says what's live.

Licensing built in

Ed25519-signed entitlements, licence keys by email, device activation and recovery. Your app verifies offline; we run the key lifecycle. T3rnel Browser sells Pro on this rail in production.

Coupons and promotions

Mint discount codes and run sale windows without redeploying — pricing is data, so a promotion is a row, not a release.

A ledger you can reconcile

Every checkout, settlement and refund in one payment feed — drain it idempotently from your backend, or read it in the console.
