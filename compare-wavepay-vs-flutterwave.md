## Questions

## Is WavePay a Flutterwave alternative?

For Zimbabwean wallets plus PayPal and crypto behind one API, yes. If your buyers are in countries WavePay's rails do not cover, a pan-African gateway may fit better.

## Can I combine them?

Not today: Flutterwave is not a WavePay rail.
