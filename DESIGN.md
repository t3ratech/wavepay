# WavePay site — direction contract

- **Product mode**: persuade — a Zimbabwe developer deciding whether this
  can take money for their software.
- **Audience**: solo devs and small studios in Zimbabwe charging for apps,
  extensions, games and tools. Pressure: dollar rails that actually settle
  locally (EcoCash/OneMoney/InnBucks/ZimSwitch) plus PayPal for export sales.
- **Visual world**: payments ledger, not crypto casino. Confidence through
  restraint — a payments surface earns trust by looking boring and precise.
- **Palette family**: WavePay teal accent on light-first paper; dark theme
  shares the accent family and goes near-black.
- **Type treatment**: system sans stack, tabular-nums on money/code,
  editorial weight only on the hero line.
- **Composition**: focused flow — one hero, one "how it works" rail, one
  pricing block, one obvious next action per screen.
- **Shape language**: 14px cards, hairline borders, minimal shadow.
- **Anti-references**: not a Stripe clone, not a gradient-heavy startup
  template, not a trading terminal. No decorative blobs or glassmorphism.
- **Locales**: en / sn / nd (T3rnel market set), theme light/dark/system
  with pre-paint resolution, agent card at `/.well-known/agent-card.json`.
