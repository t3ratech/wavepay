# Accessibility statement

WavePay aims for WCAG 2.2 level AA on every page: keyboard operation, visible focus, contrast in light and dark themes, reduced motion, right-to-left languages, and how to report a barrier.

Updated 2026-10-31 · TeraTech Solutions (Private) Limited

## Our commitment

We build this site to WCAG 2.2 level AA and check every page with an automated conformance test as well as by hand.

## What we do

- Semantic landmarks, one heading hierarchy per page, a skip link, and descriptive link text.
- Everything works with a keyboard, with visible focus and no focus traps; the search and menus close with Escape.
- Text contrast of at least 4.5:1 in light, dark and system themes; the theme is your choice and is remembered.
- Text can be resized and the layout reflows to a 320px window; touch targets are at least 44px on phones.
- Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`; images have text alternatives.
- Fifteen languages, including right-to-left Arabic, each with its own page and a language chooser that names each language in its own script.

## Known limits

- Translations other than English are machine-assisted drafts and may read awkwardly; native review is under way.
- Some payment pages belong to providers (Paynow, PayPal, 2Checkout); their accessibility is theirs to maintain.
- Code examples are text in scrollable blocks that can be copied with the Copy button.

## Tell us about a barrier

Write to [support@t3ratech.co.zw](mailto:support@t3ratech.co.zw) with the page and what went wrong. We aim to reply within five working days and to fix real barriers quickly.
