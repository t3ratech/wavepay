# WavePay vs 2Checkout (Verifone)

## Questions

## Can I use both?

Yes, once 2Checkout is enabled on your deployment: it is one of the rails behind the same checkout call.

## Who is the seller in a WavePay payment?

You are, through the provider account the money lands in. WavePay does not act as merchant of record.
